About Us

Lovely Fingers Food Mart Limited (also known as Lovely Fingers Grocery Tech Nigeria) is your premier FoodTech destination in Ikorodu, Lagos. We combine the convenience of modern technology with the warmth of traditional Nigerian market shopping. Founded by a specialty coffee operator with a passion for quality, we've built our reputation on delivering fresh groceries, premium specialty coffee, and exceptional customer service to homes and businesses across Ikorodu and beyond. Our mission is simple: to make quality food products accessible, affordable, and convenient for every Nigerian family.
